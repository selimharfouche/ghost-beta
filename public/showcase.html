From discovery to a useful report

Let Astra explore. Keep the receipts.

01 / Discover

Astra chooses the next action from the visible interface and journey context. Each Ghost has an independent session and persona.

02 / Observe

Ghost records actions, screenshots, browser errors, and optional continuous video. Model observations remain attached to their evidence.

03 / Review

Related findings are grouped into a report with reproduction steps. You decide what to fix; suspected friction stays labelled.
