## Maker comment draft

Hi Product Hunt — I built Ghost because passing tests doesn’t tell you what happens when someone meets your product for the first time.

Ghost starts with a staging URL. Separate explorers approach the app with different instincts: curious, impatient, and mobile-first. They discover their own journeys, record what happens, and bring back findings with reproduction steps and screenshot replays.

Astra makes the exploration decisions through the Codex SDK. Playwright executes the browser actions and captures evidence. Ghost runs on your machine using your own Codex login. It uses your subscription allowance; it does not require a separate API key.

The biggest thing I want feedback on: which findings would actually change what you ship? Try the planted-bug demo, inspect the evidence, and tell me where Ghost helps or misses the point.
